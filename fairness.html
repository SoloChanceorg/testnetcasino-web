  1. Before you play, the server picks a secret server seed and shows you its SHA-256 hash. It cannot change the seed later without the hash changing.

JACKPOTS

The jackpots are part of every machine's 101% Return to Player rate: every bet adds 3.36% to the machine's Minor, 2.52% to its Major and 2.52% to the shared Grand, and the head starts after a win (8.5%) are counted in it too. A spin wins them inside the game, by its own seeds: three draws after the free spins (Grand, Major, Minor, each a hit with a chance of bet / one-in), and the highest hit is won. The verifier above replays them too. What a win pays is what the jackpot holds at that moment.

  1. After a win the next cycle starts with a head start from the faucet. When a cycle starts, the server draws a secret seed and publishes its SHA-256 hash right away (under "Seed hashes now" on the jackpots page).
  2. The head start comes only from that seed: HMAC-SHA256(seed, "tnc-jackpot-meter") gives two numbers. The first picks a hidden target between the tier's minimum and maximum (small targets more likely), the second the head start, 25% to 75% of the target.
  3. The win reveals the seed. Anyone can then check that it matches the hash published before the win, that it gives the same target and head start, and that the amount won is at least the head start. While the jackpot was running only the hash was public, so the head start was fixed when the cycle started and could not be changed afterwards.

Every coin has its own jackpots and collectors. A tBTC spin's jackpot draws count its bet × 10 (tBTC amounts are a tenth of tBCH's, so the chance per spin at the same share of the maximum bet is the same); everything else is drawn exactly as for tBCH, and the Return to Player rate is 101% in both coins. tBTC jackpot targets are tBCH's divided by 10.

Older jackpot wins (from the meters that paid when they reached their target) also published a display scale; their Verify uses those rules.

ROULETTE

Every roulette round has its own secret seed. Its SHA-256 hash is published when betting opens, and the seed is revealed at the result. The seed alone fixes the winning number, the length of the spin and the golden ball: the stream above with client seed roulette and the round id as nonce gives the number (randInt(37)), the spin (28 + randInt(5) seconds), then the golden ball (randInt(236) == 0). A bet on n numbers that wins pays stake × 36 / n, × 10 in a golden round (a Return to Player rate of 101%). The golden ball is drawn when betting closes, so nobody can know it while chips are placed.

Rounds played before the golden ball (rules 1) paid stake × 37 / n with no golden ball: after the spin, every chip that covers the number, in bet order, draws the extra sat of its fraction (a Return to Player rate of 100%). Each round says which rules it was played under.

Load a round from the table by its number, or enter a seed, a round id and the round's chips yourself.
